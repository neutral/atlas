# Explanatory subjects

This Style organizes a project around durable subjects or capabilities. Each Tree
combines one subject's meaning, relevant mechanisms, choices and evidence in a
substantial account. Its Base establishes the subject's scope and principal
understanding. Tree boundaries follow the subjects readers need to understand
and maintain independently. The primary explanation of a shared mechanism or
finding belongs to one subject account; other subjects develop its consequences
for them.

Points develop the subject's broad explanation into reasons, choices, observations
and detail. A development receives its own Point when it is useful to understand,
reference or revise independently. Higher Points explain its significance and
retain the qualifications needed to understand their claims. Branches label
coherent groups within the subject. Facets explain consequential interactions
with other subjects; links provide further reading. Moving between the subject's
meaning, mechanisms and evidence develops the same account.

Atlas carries the main arguments: important choices, reasons, alternatives,
consequences and limits. Examples and counterexamples remain with an explanation
when understanding depends on them. Exhaustive procedures, code listings and raw
evidence remain in sources. Where a source owns exact requirements or procedures,
Atlas explains their reasons and consequences while preserving that source's
authority. The account summarizes relevant evidence and cites its scope, so
readers can understand the argument in Atlas and inspect its basis in the sources.

New material updates its owning explanation, with higher explanations and
connected Facets reviewed for changed meaning. New detail stays within the current
Tree while its overview can explain the subject coherently. A separate Tree is
warranted when a durable subject needs its own reader entry point and maintenance.
Restructuring addresses an identified reading or maintenance problem while
preserving conditions, reasoning and identity. It is complete when representative
questions have coherent reading paths, relevant qualifications remain visible
and the identified problem is resolved.
