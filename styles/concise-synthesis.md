# Concise synthesis

This Style organizes a project around one central account and supporting accounts.
The central Tree owns the project's integrated conclusions, bringing arguments
together to explain their combined implications, tensions and unresolved questions.
Supporting Trees own complete arguments within their scopes, including their local
conclusions. Their boundaries follow independently maintained contributions to the
integrated account. Each Tree's scope and Base establish its role and principal
understanding. The central Base develops an argument that draws on those accounts;
a directory or summary of them is insufficient.

Points explain each account's main distinctions, choices and qualifications.
Closely related explanation stays together until it needs separate reference,
assessment or revision. Central Points explain how the essential premises combine
and what qualifies the resulting conclusions. Higher Points orient readers to
their detail. Branches label useful groups of developments. Facets explain how
another account strengthens, limits or challenges a host claim; links provide
further reading. Supporting accounts can also have their own cross-account
interpretations. A Facet preserves its targets' scope and standing; its presence
supplies neither proof nor the target account's endorsement.

Atlas carries central meaning, essential reasons and qualifications in both central
and supporting accounts, including why the integrated conclusions follow. Extended
arguments, exhaustive procedures, implementation detail and raw evidence remain in
sources, with precise references explaining their contribution. Source reading is
an expected part of understanding the account in depth. An explanation expands when
readers repeatedly need to reconstruct an important connection from the underlying
material. Sources retain authority over the exact requirements or procedures they
own.

Changes update the owning explanation and its references together. Review higher
explanations, connected Facets and the central conclusions that depend on it for
changed meaning. Preserve disagreements and uncertainty where they affect a
conclusion; supporting findings remain accountable to their evidence. A new
supporting Tree is warranted when a contribution to the synthesis needs its own
account, reader entry point and maintenance. Restructuring addresses an identified
failure to find, understand or update the account, preserving identity and
important conflicts or limits. It is complete when readers can explain the central
meaning and limits, find the supporting reasoning and sources, and maintain that
path.
