# Explanatory synthesis

This Style organizes a project around one central account and supporting accounts.
The central Tree owns the project's integrated conclusions, bringing arguments
together to explain their combined implications, tensions and unresolved questions.
Supporting Trees own complete arguments within their scopes, including their local
conclusions. Their boundaries follow independently maintained contributions to the
integrated account. Each Tree's scope and Base establish its role and principal
understanding. The central Base develops an argument that draws on those accounts;
a directory or summary of them is insufficient.

Points develop each account from its broad explanation into reasons, choices,
observations and independently useful detail. Central Points explain how premises
from the supporting accounts combine and what qualifies the resulting conclusions.
Higher Points retain the conditions needed to understand their claims. Branches
label coherent groups of developments. Facets explain how another account
strengthens, limits or challenges a host claim; links provide further reading.
Supporting accounts can also have their own cross-account interpretations. A Facet
preserves its targets' scope and standing; its presence supplies neither proof nor
the target account's endorsement.

Atlas carries the main arguments in both central and supporting accounts, including
important reasons, alternatives, consequences and limits. Examples and
counterexamples remain where understanding depends on them. Exact requirements,
exhaustive procedures, code listings and raw evidence retain their source authority.
The accounts explain their significance, summarize the relevant evidence and cite
its scope. Readers can understand the integrated argument and its supporting
reasoning in Atlas, then inspect their basis in the sources.

New material updates the account that owns the affected argument. Review higher
explanations, connected Facets and the central conclusions that depend on it for
changed meaning. Preserve disagreements and uncertainty where they affect a
conclusion; supporting findings remain accountable to their evidence. A new
supporting Tree is warranted when a contribution to the synthesis needs its own
sustained argument, overview and maintenance. Restructuring addresses an identified
reading or maintenance problem while preserving reasoning and identity. It is
complete when readers can follow the integrated conclusions to their grounds and
limits, the supporting accounts remain coherent, and the identified problem is
resolved.
