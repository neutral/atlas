Working with Atlas

Start with a question, an exact id, or a source. Follow the Point’s anchor, local contexts, explained relations, and original material.

Maps and Areas
Authored questions organize context. Areas can overlap.
Points
One exact identity has one anchor and can have contexts in other Maps. Similar wording supplies candidates; it does not decide identity.
Sources
Content and typed References preserve source roles. A role establishes neither truth nor authority.
Contribute
Inspect plausible identities first. Edit, prepare, inspect the full changes, then explicitly apply. Repeated meaning needs no edit.
Drafts and saves
A stored draft remains unfinished text. Apply writes Atlas files. Draft storage keeps the current text. Discard removes the selected recovery content from the state directory.
Checks
Invoke verification explicitly. Validity, required Check coverage, actual outcomes, and truth are separate. Unavailable verification stays visible.

/ focuses search. ? opens help. Escape closes this dialog. Shortcuts leave text entry alone.

Refresh preserves draft text and its original baseline. Changes compares retained observations. A service restart expires those observations and prepared plans; durable drafts and retained reports remain.
