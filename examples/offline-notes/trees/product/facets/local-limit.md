# Editing availability has limited evidence

The [local observation](../../architecture/points/local.md) supports reopening a
saved note after a normal restart in one fictional run. The [trial's limits](../../../sources/trial.md#limits)
leave interrupted writes, crashes and power loss untested. Product can use that
result to explain a narrow part of offline continuity; it cannot turn the result
into a general promise that no writing can be lost.

This Facet attaches to **Working without a connection**, so it qualifies the
editing account as a whole. A reader of [offline work](../points/offline.md)
should assess its relevance to that claim. It supplies no evidence for the
separate [delivery wording](../points/promise.md). [Architecture's overview](../../architecture/points/purpose.md)
keeps local persistence and delivery distinct.
