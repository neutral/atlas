# Writers can keep working on notes when the connection drops

A field researcher often writes where there is no network. The product's
promise to them is continuity on the device they hold: they can write and
revise without a connection, they can see whether a note is saved, and a saved
note is there when they reopen the app.

That promise stops at the device. [Offline work stays on the current
device](offline.md) says what a writer can rely on while offline and where the
evidence for it ends. [The wording decision](promise.md) keeps a local save
apart from delivery, so the interface never suggests that a server or another
device already has the note. Product owns these expectations; [Storage and
delivery](../../architecture/points/purpose.md) explains the technical
conditions they depend on.

The account interprets a [fictional brief](../../../sources/brief.md). It
describes an intended product, one demonstration of a normal restart, and the
questions about failure that remain open.
