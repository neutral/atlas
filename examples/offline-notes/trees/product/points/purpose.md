# Keep writing when the connection drops

The intended experience lets a field researcher keep writing without waiting for
a network. The useful promise is continuity on the current device: the writer
can see whether a note has been saved and return to it after closing the app.

[Offline work](offline.md) explains that promise and its limits.
[The selected wording](promise.md) distinguishes a local save from delivery, so
the interface does not imply that another device already has the note. Product
owns those expectations; [Storage and delivery](../../architecture/points/purpose.md)
explains the technical conditions behind them.

This account interprets a [fictional brief](../../../sources/brief.md). It records
an intended product, with a narrow demonstration of normal restart and open
questions about failures. It is not a claim that a real app has shipped or that
the wording has been tested with users.
