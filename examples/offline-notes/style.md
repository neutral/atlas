# Explanatory perspectives

Develop the project through a few coherent perspectives that answer different
reader questions about shared subjects. Each Tree owns the claims and reasoning
whose meaning is best judged within its perspective. Choose the perspectives
from the work and its intended readers; Product, Engineering and Confidence are
examples, not required slots. A subject spanning perspectives does not receive a
second structural home. Place each explanation where its argument belongs, and
let each Base explain the perspective's scope and principal understanding.

Develop Points from consequential explanations into independently useful reasons,
choices, observations and details. Higher Points explain why their developments
matter, retaining the qualifications needed to read them on their own. A Branch
can group related developments when that level needs a label; use a Point when it
needs an explanation. Write a Facet when another perspective changes the meaning
of a host claim through a dependency, qualification, consequence or tension.
Ordinary further reading needs only a link. Both sides keep their own judgments;
a Facet does not stand in for the target account or make its author agree.

Carry enough explanation into Atlas for readers to understand important choices,
their reasons, consequences, alternatives and limits without reconstructing the
argument from sources. Preserve examples or counterexamples when they explain why
a claim holds. Keep exhaustive procedures, code listings and raw evidence in their
canonical sources; summarize the evidence relevant to the explanation and link to
its exact scope. Bring detail into Atlas when understanding depends on it and the
maintenance cost is justified. This style accepts a substantial explanatory
account, while source references retain the basis for inspecting and revising it.

As new material arrives, update the owning explanation and review the higher
account and connected interpretations for changed meaning. Add or split a Tree
only when a distinct perspective needs its own sustained argument, overview and
maintenance; do not mirror each new document, team or technology. Consolidate
redundant explanations without losing conditions, reasoning or stable identity.
Assess proposed restructuring against a concrete reading or maintenance problem
within this style. Stop when representative questions have coherent, appropriately
qualified paths and no consequential defect remains; another plausible outline
alone is not a reason to replace the adopted organizing policy.
