# Review citations after meaning changes

Preserve the intended meaning of known connections when a Point's scope changes.

## Requirement

After a narrowing, broadening, split, merge or replacement, review affected
placements, Facets, known citations and publication selections. Verify each citation
against its intended claim, including when IDs and paths stay the same. Publication
eligibility must remain explicit.

## Verification

Compare the former and resulting meanings. Inspect affected records and known
citing material within granted access. Record each retained, revised or removed
connection and unresolved gap. Review citations in their surrounding prose.

## Failure

Fail when a known citation silently changes meaning, a Facet no longer fits, or
publication selection expands implicitly. Report unable when the former meaning
or required citing material is unavailable.

## Exceptions

An edit that preserves existing identity and scope requires only confirmation
that this Check's trigger is absent.
