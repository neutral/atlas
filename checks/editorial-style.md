# A change follows the Atlas's own Style

## Requirement

Within the declared review scope, the change places each claim in the account
the Style says owns it, and explains it to the depth the Style sets. Higher
Points say what their developments establish together. Facets state what
another account changes about their host. The reasoning, conditions, examples
and uncertainty the Style's depth calls for are present, in the Atlas's own
words; the source's wording and outline need not be kept.

## Verification

Name the scope and read the Atlas's `style.md` in full. Read the complete
affected explanations and the permitted sources they rest on. For each
affected Point record which account the Style assigns it to and whether its
depth matches. For a substantial creation or rebuild, rehearse one realistic
update: find the owning explanation, the Points above it and the Facets that
bear on it, and confirm that a changed finding can be brought in without
losing its limits. For a proposed restructure, name the reading or upkeep
problem it solves and how that will be judged; another plausible outline is
not a reason.

## Failure

Report `fail` for a claim explained in full in more than one account, a depth
at odds with the Style, a grouping no Point explains, a lost qualification, or
a restructure justified only by preference. Report `unable` when the scope or
the required sources cannot be examined.

## Exceptions

A narrow review passes only for its declared scope. It does not establish that
the whole Atlas follows its Style or that readers are better served.
