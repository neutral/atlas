# Review understanding within the adopted Style

## Requirement

For the declared review scope, assess whether the Atlas develops useful,
appropriately qualified explanations within its complete adopted Style. Identify
the exact Style and candidate being reviewed. The core's ownership, identity,
Facet and evidence boundaries remain in force. Review a legacy Atlas against its
explicitly declared reader questions; do not invent an adopted Style or claim
Style conformity until a definition has been deliberately adopted.

Higher Points must explain the significance of their detail. A Point may make a
useful contribution through a faithful summary or orientation; original reasoning
beyond its sources is not required. Facets must explain consequential
interpretations of their hosts. Keep the reasoning, conditions, examples and
uncertainty needed by the adopted source-depth policy, preserving meaning without
requiring the source's wording or outline.

## Verification

Record representative reader questions and an explicit review scope, then read
the complete affected explanations and relevant permitted sources. Record observed
paths, answer boundaries, source dependencies and concrete defects. Include one
realistic update rehearsal when reviewing a substantial creation or rebuild:
identify the owning explanation, relevant ancestors and interpretations, and
verify that a changed finding can be incorporated without losing its limits.

For a proposed restructure, identify the concrete reading or maintenance problem,
the expected benefit within the selected Style and how it will be assessed.
Another plausible organization is insufficient. Stop when the declared questions
have coherent paths, sources supply the intended level of detail, and no
consequential defect in scope remains. Passing requires reasons and observations
for the actual candidate, not a count of Points, link coverage or a validator's
acceptance. Comparative usefulness requires separate reader evidence.

## Failure

Report `fail` for consequential unexplained groupings, lost qualifications,
unsupported interpretations, source-depth or ownership decisions inconsistent with
the adopted Style, or restructuring justified only by preference. Report `unable`
when required sources or review scope cannot be examined. Preserve unresolved
findings and distinguish unreviewed material from a demonstrated pass.

## Exceptions

Record deliberate exclusions and their consequences with the result. A narrow
review can pass only for its declared scope; it cannot claim that the entire Atlas
was reviewed or that unaided readers benefit. This advisory Check adds review
depth and does not override core requirements or the selected Style.
